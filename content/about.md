## About me

Co-founder of <a href="https://antistatique.net">Antistatique</a>, a swiss web agency in Lausanne, and Geneva (and in Miami for a few years).

With over =(time() - 1998) years of web experience, I have carried out and managed all types of projects in the field. Since I have experience working across all parts of the media production chain, I can take on many different roles when it comes to designing, developing, project management, and quality control. I possess a clear vision of what is required for each project, thanks to my expertise in human factors and ergonomics, usability, accessibility, and technology.

I have a unique vision on technology and teach laboratory courses in the Media Engineering section at HEIG-VD University of Applied Science, with a focus on technological watchfulness as the main topic. Additionally, I sometimes give [talks](../talking/) about the web, its evolution, web standards, interaction design, and internationalization.
I believe that using the lowest technology layer to solve a given problem is often the best choice, and I'm obsessed with creating simpler solutions and reducing maintenance overhead.

My interests include Design, CSS, Documentation, Web Standards, A11y, and I18n.
